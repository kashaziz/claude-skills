## ✅ What You'll Get

Every content piece includes:

### 📊 SEO Package
- Optimized title tag (50-60 chars)
- Meta description (150-160 chars)
- Primary & secondary keywords
- URL slug suggestion

### 📝 Main Content
- Headline with keyword
- Structured headers (H2, H3)
- Natural keyword integration
- Engaging, human-like writing
- Clear call-to-action

### 🔗 Optimization Tips
- Internal linking suggestions
- External source recommendations
- Image suggestions with alt text
- Schema markup ideas
- Readability metrics

## 🚨 Common Mistakes to Avoid

### ❌ Don't: "Write a blog post about halal food"
**Too vague** - Claude needs more context

### ✅ Do: "Create a 1200-word blog post about verifying halal status of food additives, targeting Muslim families who grocery shop weekly, with goal of ranking for 'how to check halal ingredients' keyword"
**Specific and actionable**

---

### ❌ Don't: "Make it sound good"
**Subjective and unclear**

### ✅ Do: "Use conversational tone like Headspace app, avoid corporate jargon, write at 8th-grade reading level"
**Clear parameters**

---

### ❌ Don't: Skip the discovery questions
**You'll get generic content**

### ✅ Do: Answer thoughtfully
**Better input = exceptional output**
